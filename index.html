Why it helps

Quota is easier as a calendar.

Raw percentages are hard to pace. Codex Quota Calendar makes the remaining weekly window feel like a daily budget you can actually act on.

◔

Daily share

See how much of today's average share has been used, with a simple progress ring.

▦

Remaining days

Future days stay simple: unused days show zero, while today carries the actual usage.

⌁

Smoother speed

A weighted average over recent local samples makes runout estimates less jumpy.

⌘

Menu bar native

Built with SwiftUI as a compact macOS menu bar utility, not a web wrapper.

Privacy

Local by default. Bounded by design.

Auth data stays on this Mac. The app does not run a server, send analytics, or upload your usage history. The local history log is intentionally small so it can help estimate pace without becoming a data sink.
